About NK Architects

Excellence in architectural design since our founding

Our Vision

NK Architects is a leading architectural firm based in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. We specialize in creating innovative, sustainable, and culturally responsive designs that shape the future of Ethiopian architecture.

Our team combines international best practices with deep local knowledge to deliver projects that are both beautiful and functional, respecting the rich heritage of Ethiopia while embracing modern design principles.
